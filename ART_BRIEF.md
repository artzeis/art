# Myth Seekers — Character Art Brief

This brief is for an illustrator or an AI image tool (ChatGPT, Gemini, Midjourney and so on). It produces
professional, realistic artwork for the six characters. Finished pictures drop straight into the game: see
[Adding the pictures to the game](#adding-the-pictures-to-the-game).

## What every picture needs
- **One character per picture, full body**, standing or sitting, centred, facing slightly toward the viewer.
- **Transparent background** (PNG). If the tool can't do that, use a plain white background and remove it
  afterwards: on iPhone, touch and hold the subject in Photos and choose "Copy Subject"; you can also use
  Canva's "BG Remover" or remove.bg.
- **Portrait shape**, about **1200 × 1500 px**.
- **Same style, lighting and camera angle for all six**, so they look like one cast.
- **Made for children:** friendly expressions, warm and magical, nothing scary. Clothing is modest and
  age-appropriate.

## Style — pick one for the whole cast
Paste the **style line** at the start of every character prompt. These are five "versions of the gang";
make a full set in one style before trying another.

1. **Collectible doll (recommended):** *Photorealistic premium collectible fantasy doll, soft vinyl and
   fabric textures, hand-painted details, glossy realistic eyes, studio product photography, soft
   key light and gentle rim light, shallow depth of field, ultra detailed, 8k*
2. **Live-action film:** *Cinematic still from a live-action family fantasy film, realistic actor in
   professional costume and makeup, natural skin texture, magical soft lighting, shot on 85mm, ultra detailed*
3. **Animated feature film:** *High-end 3D animated feature film character, expressive and appealing,
   subsurface skin shading, detailed hair and fabric, cinematic studio lighting, ultra detailed render*
4. **Porcelain doll:** *Antique-style porcelain fairy-tale doll, painted bisque face, real fabric costume
   with fine stitching, museum display lighting, photorealistic macro photography*
5. **Storybook painting:** *Lush professional children's-book oil painting, rich brushwork, warm glowing
   light, highly detailed, classic fairy-tale illustration quality*

## Character prompts
Use: **[style line]** + the character text + *"full body, centred, plain white background, no text"*.

**1. Pip Petalwing — Fairy** → save as `fairy.png`

**3. Lirien Leafwhisper — Elf** → save as `elf.png`

> cloak over a pale green tunic with a leaf-shaped brooch and a brown leather belt. A silver longbow

**4. Ember Sunfeather — Sunfeather Bird** → save as `bird.png`

**5. Thistle Swiftmane — Centaur** → save as `centaur.png`

**6. Leo Starpaw — Moonhusky** → save as `leo.png`

## Adding the pictures to the game
1. Name the files exactly: `fairy.png`, `gnome.png`, `elf.png`, `bird.png`, `centaur.png`, `leo.png`.
2. Put them in the **`assets/characters/`** folder, either by uploading them on GitHub (open the folder,
   then **Add file → Upload files**) or by sending them to Claude to add.
3. The game uses them automatically everywhere: cards, creature pages, quests and AR. Any character
   without a picture keeps its current drawing, so you can add them one at a time.
