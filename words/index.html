How to play Wordlings

  1. Make a word. Slide your finger from letter to letter — any direction, even diagonally — or tap them one by one. Words need 3 or more letters, and each bubble can be used once per word.
  2. Pop! Tap Play. The bubbles you used pop, the letters above drop down, and new letters float in from the top. The board keeps changing!
  3. Score. Rare letters are worth more. ⭐ Star bubbles double the whole word, 🌙 Moon bubbles triple their letter, and 💎 Gem bubbles add 5. Long words get a bonus: 5 letters +3, 6 letters +6, 7 letters +10, 8 or more +15.
  4. Dream Power. Everyone plays with a Dreamling partner, and each Dreamling has one special power you can use once per game.
  5. Stuck? 💡 Hint shows the start of a good word (3 per game). 🌀 Shake mixes up the board but skips your turn.
  6. Win. After the last round, the highest score wins!
