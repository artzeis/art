How to Play

Seven squishy friends have lost their treasures. Let's help them!

  1. 🔑
    Grown-ups hide the treasures. Open Grown-ups and hide the creature accessories inside, outside, or both: Scribbi's Rainbow Crayon, Craggle's Glow Crystal, Orbit's Moon Marble, Tangle's Golden Key, Nyxie's Wishing Star, Glitch's Power Pixel and Leo's Moon Medallion. Choose where you're playing, then type where you hid each one — the app writes the riddles.
  2. 👆
    Pick a creature who needs help.
  3. 🏃
    Spin for a challenge — Physical, Mental, Teamwork or Kindness — and turn the dial from Easy to Super. Harder challenges earn bonus ⭐!
  4. 🧩
    Solve the riddle. It tells you where to look. Stuck? Tap Clue, then Big hint.
  5. 🎁
    Find it! Show the creature what you found to earn ⭐ stars and unlock secret facts.
  6. 📜
    Treasure trails. Grown-ups can add clue stops, so each riddle leads to a clue card, then the next, then the treasure. More running, more fun!
  7. 🎨
    Make something together. After the treasure, each character asks you to build or draw something. Snap a photo for your Memory Book!
  8. 🌙
    Calm down with Nyxie. Finish with 2 minutes of slow stretches and moon breathing.
  9. 📷
    AR Hunt (optional). First scan the room with the camera so the creature can pick a hiding spot. Then turn slowly, follow the 🔥 meter, and tap the creature when you see it peeking out!

🛡️ Grown-ups: keep hiding spots safe and at kid height, and supervise AR mode — watch where you walk while looking at a screen.
