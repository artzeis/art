How to Play

  1. Grown-up setup. Open Grown-ups, then hide each of the five included creature accessories around your play area — the Flower Crown, Pointy Red Hat, Silver Leaf Bow, Golden Feather and Lucky Horseshoe. Tell the app where you hid each one.
  2. Pick a creature. On the Home screen, kids choose a creature who has lost something.
  3. Do the challenge. Every creature asks for an activity first — flap, gallop, tiptoe or listen — to prove you're a real Myth Seeker.
  4. Solve the riddle. The riddle points to the hiding spot. Stuck? Tap Clue, then Big Hint.
  5. Find it! Bring back the accessory and answer the creature's question. You'll earn ⭐ Sparkle Stars and the creature joins your Creature Journal.
  6. Optional AR mode. Tap AR Mode to look through the camera. Creatures hide around you — turn slowly and follow the warm/cold meter, then tap a creature to play and learn.

🛡️ Grown-ups: keep hiding spots safe and at kid height, and supervise AR mode — watch where you walk while looking through a screen!
