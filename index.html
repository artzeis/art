How to Play

  1. 🔑
    Grown-ups hide the treasures. Open Grown-ups and hide the five creature accessories around your play area: the Flower Crown, Pointy Red Hat, Silver Leaf Bow, Golden Feather and Lucky Horseshoe. Tell the app where you hid each one.
  2. 👆
    Pick a creature. Choose a friend who has lost something.
  3. 🏃
    Do the challenge. Flap, gallop, tiptoe or listen to prove you're a real Myth Seeker.
  4. 🧩
    Solve the riddle. It tells you where to look. Stuck? Tap Clue, then Big Hint.
  5. 🎁
    Find it! Bring it back and show the creature. Earn ⭐ stars and unlock secret fun facts.
  6. 🔮
    AR mode (optional). Look through the camera, turn slowly and follow the 🔥 meter to find creatures hiding around you. Tap them to play!

🛡️ Grown-ups: keep hiding spots safe and at kid height, and supervise AR mode — watch where you walk while looking through a screen!
