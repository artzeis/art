      How to play

      • Tap or click a square to select it. Tap it again to switch between Across and Down.
      • Type to fill letters — the cursor moves along the word for you.
      • Arrow keys move around, Space switches direction, Tab jumps to the next clue, Backspace erases.
      • Click a clue in the list to jump straight to it.
      • Stuck? Use Check to spot mistakes or Reveal for a nudge.
      • Your progress saves automatically — come back any time.
