        How to play

        • Tap or click a square to select it; tap again to switch between Across and Down. Type to fill letters.
        • Keyboard: arrows move, Space switches direction, Tab jumps to the next clue, Backspace erases.
        • Modes: Classic tracks your time, Zen hides the clock, and Time Attack is a countdown where every solved word adds seconds.
        • Sparks ✦ are earned as you solve. Spend them on power-ups: Anagram shows a word's letters scrambled, Flip clue swaps to an alternate clue, Hint letter fills a square, Freeze stops the countdown.
        • Combos: solve words quickly in a row for bonus XP.
        • Every solved word goes into your Wordbook on the Profile page. Share your result when you finish!
